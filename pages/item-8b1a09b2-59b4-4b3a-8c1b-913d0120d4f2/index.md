Every penalty in the game comes from the USA Hockey rulebook. When the ref calls one, the game stops for a moment to show the ref's signal and explain the rule.

The rule

How it happens in the game

Tripping

Using your stick, knee or foot to make another player fall.

Stick play on the puck carrier.

Hooking

Using the blade of your stick to pull or hold back another player.

Stick play on the puck carrier.

Slashing

Swinging your stick at another player, even if you only hit their stick or gloves.

Stick play, and once in a while a poke check.

Holding

Grabbing another player or their stick to slow them down.

Stick play, on or off the puck.

Interference

Hitting, holding or blocking a player who does not have the puck.

A check or stick play on someone without the puck.

High-sticking

Carrying your stick above your shoulders and making contact with another player.

Stick play.

Cross-checking

Hitting another player with the shaft of your stick held in both hands.

A body check from behind.

Roughing

Extra pushing, shoving or punching, usually after the whistle.

An occasional rough body check.

Body checking

Body checking is not allowed until 14U. Younger players learn body contact and angling first, so they are ready for it.

Any body check below 14U.

Fighting

Dropping the gloves earns a game misconduct.

AAA & Beer League only. Both fighters are ejected, and when a team runs out of players the game is called.

Calls, delays and power plays
-----------------------------

*   **Delayed penalties**: the ref raises an arm and waits until the team that committed the penalty touches the puck.
*   **Power plays**: a minor is two minutes in the box, and a power play goal ends it early.
*   **Offsides and icing** are called on full ice from 14U up, and never on roller.
*   **Ref strictness**: the slider on the setup screen scales every call. Jay mode turns penalties off entirely.
