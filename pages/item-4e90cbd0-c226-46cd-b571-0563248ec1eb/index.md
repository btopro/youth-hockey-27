Five teams, each with home and away kits. All the rosters are made up: invented names and invented numbers.

*   ![](files/crests/sparkle-selects.svg) **AAAA Sparkle Selects**: neon pink, with yellow and blue.
*   ![](files/crests/b-squad.svg) **B Squad**: black and pink.
*   ![](files/crests/jesus-selects.svg) **Jesus Selects**: white and royal blue with gold trim, a halo over the puck.
*   ![](files/crests/elite-prospects.svg) **Elite Prospects**: black, gold and silver. The crown came with the registration fee.
*   ![](files/crests/d1-or-bust.svg) **D1 or Bust**: blaze orange and navy. The plan is already on the jersey.

Every team carries 13 skaters and 2 goalies. Each player has ratings for skating, shooting, passing, stickhandling, checking and stamina, plus coachability and exposure. You can read how those last two work in [behind the game](behind-the-game).

Guest stars
-----------

A few cheat codes put a guest star on your team: someone who drives the ice machine, someone who brought the orange slices, a tall hairy legend and the team mascot. The codes are hinted on the game's HOW TO PLAY screen.
