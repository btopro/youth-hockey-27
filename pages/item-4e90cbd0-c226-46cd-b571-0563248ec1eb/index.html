Four teams, each with home and away kits. All the rosters are made up: invented names and invented numbers.

  • AAAA Sparkle Selects: neon pink, with yellow and blue.
  • B Squad: black and pink.
  • Saber Cats: black and electric green.
  • Icers: navy and white.

Every team carries 13 skaters and 2 goalies. Each player has ratings for skating, shooting, passing, stickhandling, checking and stamina, plus coachability and exposure. You can read how those last two work in behind the game.
