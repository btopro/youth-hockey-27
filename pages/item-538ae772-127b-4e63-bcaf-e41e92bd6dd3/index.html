One player can play on the keyboard. Up to four players can play with gamepads: on the setup screen, push left or right on a gamepad to move it to a team, and press A or Start to drop the puck. The CPU plays everyone else.

Setting up a game

  • Teams: AAAA Sparkle Selects, B Squad, Saber Cats and the Icers.
  • Age level: 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, 18U, or AAA & Beer League. See age levels for what changes.
  • Ice or roller: roller is 4 on 4 on a full rink with no offsides, no icing and no body checking.
  • Exhibition or tournament: a tournament is a four-team bracket where the games you are not in are simulated. At 8U and 10U it is a jamboree instead: three games, no bracket, a medal for everyone.
  • Referee: the strictness slider sets how often the ref calls what they see. Jay mode calls nothing at all.

Stick play is a gamble

The stick button can slow down the puck carrier or knock the puck loose, and you might get a penalty for it. Using it on the player with the puck rarely gets called. Using it on someone without the puck usually does. Below 14U, a body check is always a penalty.

Between periods

Something happens at every intermission: a parent with an envelope, a hallway interview, a showcase brochure, a fight in the stands, or orange slices. What you choose changes the rest of the game.

Sharing a great play

Press R, or the Save clip button, and the game records the last 10 seconds as a video you can download and send to your fans.
