The menus work with the arrow keys and Enter, or with any gamepad: up and down move, left and right change a setting, A selects and B goes back. One player can play on the keyboard and up to four on gamepads. The CPU plays everyone else.

Setting up a game
-----------------

*   **Teams**: AAAA Sparkle Selects, B Squad, Jesus Selects, Elite Prospects and D1 or Bust. See [the teams](teams).
*   **Age level**: 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, 18U, or AAA & Beer League. See [age levels](age-levels) for what changes. Every period is five minutes at every level.
*   **Venue**: the rink; roller (4 on 4, no offsides, no icing, no body checking); pond hockey; or street hockey. At the pond and in the street there are no refs and nobody comes to watch, so every kid plays a lot better. The pond has no goalies, just tiny nets.
*   **Exhibition or tournament**: a tournament is a bracket where the games you are not in are simulated. At 8U and 10U it is a jamboree instead: three games, no bracket, a medal for everyone.
*   **Referee**: the strictness setting is how often the ref calls what they see. Jay mode calls nothing at all.

Stick play is a gamble
----------------------

The stick button can slow down the puck carrier or knock the puck loose, and you might get a penalty for it. Using it on the player with the puck rarely gets called. Using it on someone without the puck usually does. Below 14U, a body check is always a penalty.

When the ref misses one
-----------------------

A penalty the ref doesn't call can start a fight in the stands between the two kids' parents. You play your kid's parent, boxing-game style: hold left or right to dodge, down to block, Z to jab and X to hook. Dodge a punch and counter for a star, then C throws the star punch. Nobody wins: the crowd sours, both kids are rattled and both parents are banned. You can turn brawls off in OPTIONS.

One stick breaks every game
---------------------------

Once a game, a $349 composite stick snaps, and you get a close-up. The kid has no stick until the next whistle.

Between periods
---------------

First a parent opens BookFace to post highlights from the period. Pick the clips. The post never matches them: if the team is losing, Billy has played a lot today; up by a goal, the coaches are amazing. Then something else happens: a parent with an envelope, a hallway interview, a showcase brochure, a fight in the stands, or orange slices. What you choose changes the rest of the game.

Cheat codes
-----------

Type codes on the EXTRAS screen to unlock guest stars, equipment and game changers like big heads. The game's HOW TO PLAY screen has hints. Old gamers know a certain button code works on any menu.

Sharing a great play
--------------------

Press R, or the Save clip button, and the game records the last 10 seconds as a video you can download and send to your fans.
